## Meet Karl Herzberg 

For Karl, craft beer is a hands-on labor of love that all started with a simple
Christmas gift: a Mr. Beer homebrew kit. That single holiday batch sparked a
lifelong obsession with the science and craft of fermentation, eventually
leading him to the local homebrew club in Danville. It was there, swapping
recipes and sharing pints, that he met his future business partners, Herb Atwell
and John Andorfer.

Today, as a co-founder and one of the brewers at Mountain Valley Public House,
Karl brings years of local brewing expertise and a relentless DIY spirit to
Danville’s craft beer scene. His deep roots in the regional beer community
include serving as the head brewer at Mountain Valley Brewing and lending his
talents to the team at Renewal Brewing in Martinsville.

Karl believes that great beer—and a great business—is built from the ground up.
With a professional background as a handyman, he is just as comfortable fixing a
cooling pump, fabricating taproom fixtures, or troubleshooting equipment as he
is formulating a grain bill. This unique blend of technical problem-solving and
craftsmanship ensures that everything from the brewhouse to the glass is running
flawlessly.

Whether he is meticulously dialing in a new recipe, managing operations with
Herb and John, or working his weekly shift behind the bar, Karl is dedicated to
creating an unforgettable experience.

When he isn’t managing production, fixing things around the pub, or serving
pints, you can usually find him experimenting with new flavors in the kitchen or
managing a beautifully chaotic pack of 12 dogs 16 pigs 8 goats 4 sheep along
with some chickens and turkeys at home.

Stop by the public house at 592 Monument Street, grab a seat at the bar, and say
hello.
