## Commitment to Quality

Mountain Valley Public House is deeply committed to producing quality beer. Both
of its owner-brewers are passionate about the beer-making process. Both Karl and
John have been brewing for over a decade, and over that time their knowledge of
the process has been honed by the hundreds of recipes they have brewed. They
both appreciate the complexity that is possible even though beer is made from
four simple ingredients. Karl and John have different approaches to beer-making.
Karl is an experimentalist who loves to push the boundaries of what is possible,
and John is a trained research scientist who loves balancing the science and art
of brewing beer. Their different styles synergize well and help make MVPH's beer
an exceptional product.
