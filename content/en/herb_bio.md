## Herb's Bio

Herb is a native Virginian, born in Richmond and raised in Lynchburg. He studied
engineering at Virginia Tech before shifting paths and earning his Master's
Electrician title at age 27. From there, he built a successful electrical
contracting business in Northern Virginia, then dove into computers and
software—building PCs, troubleshooting IT issues, and eventually working in
controls technology, including lightning detection systems. His experience spans
semiconductor safety controls, data centers and even real estate!

In 2012, Herb met his now-wife, Peggy, and together they embarked on the
unpredictable path of craft beer. What began as homebrewing experiments quickly
grew into a passion. In 2014, they moved to Axton, Virginia, started a small hop
farm, and soon after opened **Mountain Valley Brewing**—a beloved farm brewery
that became a local destination.

After eight successful years, with Karl Herzberg as innovative brewer and
handyman extraordinaire, the couple made the difficult decision to close the
brewery in 2025. But the spirit of Mountain Valley lives on.

Today, Herb carries that legacy forward with Mountain Valley Public House, where
you can once again enjoy the same high-quality brews, now with Karl Herzberg and
John Andorfer at the helm.
